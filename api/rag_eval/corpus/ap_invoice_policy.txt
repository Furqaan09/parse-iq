Accounts Payable Invoice Matching Policy

Three-Way Match
Before an invoice is paid, Accounts Payable compares it with the purchase order and the goods receipt.
The invoiced quantity must not exceed the quantity recorded as received.
The invoiced unit price must be within 2% of the purchase order unit price, unless a signed amendment sets a new price.
Line totals must add up to the invoice total.

Approvals
Invoices over $25,000 need approval from the Controller before payment.
Invoices that fail the three-way match are placed on hold until the difference is explained.

Supplier Bank Details
A change to a supplier's bank details is never accepted by email alone.
Accounts Payable must confirm the change by calling the supplier at the phone number already on file.
