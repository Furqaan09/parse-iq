Master Supply Agreement MSA-2026-014

Between Calder Logistics Inc. (the Buyer) and Northpeak Technologies Ltd. (the Supplier).
Effective date: January 15, 2027. Term: two years.

Products and Pricing
SKU LT-200 Laptop, 14-inch: $840.00 per unit
SKU DK-15 Docking Station: $185.00 per unit
Prices are fixed for the term and may change only through a written amendment signed by both parties.

Ordering and Delivery
The Buyer orders by issuing a purchase order that references this agreement.
The Supplier must deliver within 10 business days of receiving a purchase order.
Partial shipments are allowed. Any backorder must be confirmed to the Buyer in writing with an expected ship date.

Payment Terms
Payment terms are Net 30 from the date a correct invoice is received.
Invoices must quote the purchase order number and list each SKU, quantity and unit price.
